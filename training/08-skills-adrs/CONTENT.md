# Module 08: Skills & ADRs

## Overview
How to map your Architectural Decision Records (ADRs) to Copilot Skills to ensure the AI never "forgets" a decision.

## The Mapping
1. **Decision**: "We will use Cosmos DB for all user-generated content."
2. **ADR**:

## Workflow
- Every time a new ADR is approved, update the corresponding Skill file.
- This ensures the AI's "instructions" always match the current "architecture."
